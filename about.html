About TriProof AI

Trust and authenticity for every unit, from factory to buyer.

Our Mission

TriProof AI exists to stop the counterfeit goods that erode trust between Bangladeshi factories and the global buyers who depend on the products they make. Every unit is signed with a cryptographic signature under a quota-locked purchase order, so authenticity stays provable at the point of sale even without a network connection. Our tools turn that single signature into a full trust story: offline verification, clone-scan detection, precision recalls, rework lineage, and compliance guidance in the buyer's language.

How It Works

The product runs on three steps. Each step reuses the same signed unit, so trust is never rebuilt by hand.

Sign

Sign

A purchase order locks the unit into a quota and the scanner attaches an Ed25519 signature that only that order can authorise.

Verify

Verify

Any buyer or auditor verifies the label instantly, online or offline, and the system flags clone scans in real time.

Audit / Recall

Audit / Recall

When a batch is suspect, TriProof runs a precision recall, reconstructs rework lineage, and issues bilingual compliance guidance in seconds.
